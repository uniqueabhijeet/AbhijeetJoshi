07

The Way I See Her

She has a lot of love to give. Maybe she has no energy left to put it out there. Maybe she is simply scared. I don't know which it is yet.

She will expand you and herself into what you are meant to be when you are in love.

There is a room full of her actual needs — not just the needs she believes she is allowed to have. But there is no door for her to connect to that room. She is alone in there. Not the kind of alone that means empty — just the kind where no one has been able to reach her yet.

She has good mum qualities and good teacher qualities. She is shrewd and intelligent, but her intelligence is often overlooked — sometimes even by herself.

She confuses her selflessness with self-esteem.

She is so much more than a pretty face. I love that she is kind apparently for no reason — rescuing and caring for an abandoned cat even though she hates cats, being polite, and providing meals to cleaning staff. Her soul is just as pretty as her face.

She has a very childlike energy — rabbit walks, penguin walks, mimicking a cat's voice, laughing like a horse, childhood games, nursery poems and enacting things.

♥

She once told me that the journey so far has been so beautiful that she could spend the rest of her life with these memories with me.

She is scared. And I think she knows that if she lets herself be happy for just a moment, she might feel like the whole world is going to come crashing down.

She is the kind of person who makes everything more complicated than it needs to be.

And somehow, that is both the curse and the compliment.
